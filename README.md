"The World Today" is a one-image newspaper front page summing up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026, drawn entirely by a Python program (standard library only, hand-rolled PNG encoder and bitmap font, fully deterministic). The lead story is Ukraine's sea-drone attack that sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi port in Libya; inside panels cover the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya with no deal, a Russian strike killing 11 near Kyiv, the AI sell-off rattling world markets (Nikkei -5.1%, S&P 500 -3.7%), and U.S. Democrats winning their biggest House majority since 1992, plus a ticker with the Sudan drone attacks on Khartoum, the Banten candy factory fire in Indonesia, Trump's Iran warning, and Alcaraz's first ATP Finals title.
